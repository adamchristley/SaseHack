Our goal

Our goal is to make every dollar a stydent qualifies for visible in one place, before the deadline passes.

None of this money is hidden on purpose. Universities publish their awards, bursars publish their fee schedules, and the businesses around campus run student rates. It is all public. It is spread across forty pages nobody has time to read, written in language nobody teaches you.

So we do not create money and we do not guess. We index what your campus already publishes, match it against who you actually are, and show you the source for every figure on the screen. If we cannot link to where a number came from, we do not show it.
